Introduce your work for review

The existing public intake accepts Practitioner, Service or Place suggestions. It does not have a separate organisation registration type.

Your request goes to Lumeya’s private editorial queue. An operator checks sources and your relationship to the work, asks for clarification if needed, then explicitly approves the public fields. Only approved information is published.

Describe the public scope, format and who provides it. Do not include client details, health information or records. Use an email address, Telegram handle or phone number. This follow-up contact remains private; any public listing contact is reviewed separately. Claiming to be a provider does not establish identity.

To update existing information, use “Suggest a correction” on its listing. Sending a correction never edits the public record automatically. If a receipt cannot be confirmed, retry the unchanged details here; a retry reuses the same request key.
