Dated events and undated formats

Events

Upcoming scheduled dates are loaded separately from recurring ideas and explanatory formats. A format card never implies that an event is booked.

A scheduled card reflects the public event record and its current date. Event formats describe a way to meet or work together; they do not have a published date unless one appears in the scheduled section.
