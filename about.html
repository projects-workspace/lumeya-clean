How Lumeya works

Discovery with context

Lumeya connects people with holistic and nature-oriented services, practitioners, organisations, places, events and related approaches—including aligned ecological and craft work.

One connected relationship

People → Services → Practitioners → Places → Events → Relationships

Discover

Start from a service, provider, place, event format or topic instead of guessing the right category.

Understand

Read published scope, status, format, location and related work before choosing a contact action.

Locate and connect

Use honest location precision and the provider’s published contact route. A contact request does not confirm a booking.

Information and curation

A suggestion enters review; it does not become a public listing automatically. Unknown details stay unknown, undated formats stay separate from scheduled events, and unverified locations do not receive map pins.

Source status

Public cards reflect information currently available to Lumeya and link to an existing source page when one is supplied. Missing fields are marked “Not published”.

Trust without overclaiming

Publication is not independent verification, professional licensing, certification or a guarantee of outcomes. Provider declarations and checked information must remain distinct.

Minimal data

Public requests collect only the context needed for curation or follow-up. Do not submit client details, medical records or sensitive health information.

Current and future

A public discovery foundation

The current site is a guest-only directory and request surface. It does not treat a suggestion as a published listing or a request as a confirmed service.

Available in this build

Public service, provider, place and event-format pages; category navigation; verified-coordinate map rules; and no-account public request forms.

Still future work

Provider Space, self-service publishing, accounts, certification, booking, payments, messaging and cross-project data sharing are not active.

For providers

A curated listing can connect a real offer with its provider, place and public contact details. Lumeya does not promise audience size, leads or commercial results.

Shape the network

Introduce a practitioner, service or place for curation, or tell Lumeya what you are looking for. Requests require no account; online submission depends on the public request service being available.
